The world's best talent
shouldn't be stopped
by paperwork

In 2020, I was finishing school and supposed to fly to the US on an exchange program. Then COVID happened, and the trip didn't.

But the dream didn't go anywhere either.

Over the next few years I found myself in product design — started working, got better, kept going. At some point I decided to stop waiting and apply for an O-1A visa on my own.

I got an RFE. My first petition wasn't quite right. But that RFE turned out to be the best thing that could have happened — it forced me to understand exactly how these cases work, what USCIS is actually looking for, and how to build a petition that holds up.

I got my visa.

Then I started helping others. What I kept seeing was the same thing: talented people who didn't believe they were talented enough. Engineers, founders, designers — people who had already built something real, but thought the visa was for someone else.

That's what Apex is about. No paperwork should keep you from the life or the work you're meant to do.

Bermet D.
Founder of Apex · O-1A visa holder
