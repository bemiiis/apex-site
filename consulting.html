Three visas for extraordinary people

Tech · Business · Science

The most common path for founders, engineers, and researchers. Temporary work visa, typically 3 years — renewable.

Get started
01Awards — Hackathons, grants, or acceptance into top-tier accelerators
02Membership — Exclusive tech communities, founder clubs, or expert groups
03Press — Articles about you or your product in specialized or major media
04Original contributions — An algorithm, product, or system with proven industry impact
05Scholarly articles — Technical papers, reviews, or docs in peer-reviewed publications
06Judging — Hackathon juries, PR reviews, or vetting technical projects
07Critical role — Lead / Founding Engineer / CTO / CEO in a distinguished organization
08High salary — Compensation significantly above market average for your role

Arts · Film · Entertainment

For directors, designers, musicians, and creators with a body of work. If your work has real recognition, you likely qualify.

Get started
01Lead role — Leading or starring role in a major product rollout or distinguished project
02Critical acclaim — Articles and case reviews about your work in major industry media
03Commercial success — Products you designed achieved massive adoption, top rankings, or revenue
04Significant recognition — Endorsements from established industry leaders
05High salary — Compensation substantially above average in your creative field
06Past achievements — Track record evidenced by critical reviews or major industry assets

Permanent Residency

Same extraordinary ability standard as O-1A — but permanent. No employer, no sponsor, no waiting for a visa number. You stay.

Get started
01Awards — Major prizes, grants, or top placements at a national or international level
02Membership — Selective associations that require outstanding achievements for entry
03Press — Dedicated articles, interviews, and features about you in major media
04Judging — Contest juries, hackathon judging, or reviewing peer research
05Original contributions — Systems, algorithms, or patents with proven major industry impact
06Scholarly articles — Analytical or technical articles in peer-reviewed publications
07Critical role — Essential role in companies with a distinguished reputation
08High salary — Income substantially exceeding the market average
09Exhibitions — Work displayed at major scientific, artistic, or professional showcases
10Commercial success — Proven results via downloads, active users, or revenue generated

Four steps to filing

Step 1

Qualification Call (30 min)

We look honestly at your background and tell you whether you have a case. If you need more work, we give you a clear roadmap.

Step 2

Case Architecture (1 week)

We lock in your 3 strongest criteria and design a strategy around them. You'll know the exact plan before we write a single word.

Step 3

Content Drafting (3–6 weeks)

We write the petition, draft recommendation templates, and complete all forms. You provide the facts; we build the legal argument.

Step 4

Filing & Defense

We file your petition and handle any RFE responses at no extra cost. We stay with you until your Approval Notice arrives.
