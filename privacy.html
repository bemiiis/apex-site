Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Apex ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Contact information you provide (name, email, phone number)
  • Professional background information you share during consultations
  • Communications between you and Apex
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, browser type) via analytics

How we use it

  • To evaluate your visa eligibility and provide consulting services
  • To communicate with you about your case
  • To improve our website and services
  • We do not sell your data to third parties

Data storage

Your information is stored securely. We retain it only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

Third-party services

We use trusted third-party tools (such as email and scheduling services) to operate our business. These providers have their own privacy policies and handle your data according to their terms.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, email us at bermet@runapex.co.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at bermet@runapex.co.

Apex is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We provide visa preparation and consulting services. All information on this website is for informational purposes only.
